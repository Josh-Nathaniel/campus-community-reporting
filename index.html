Common Campus Issues You Can Report

🚰 Facilities

Leaking faucets, broken water fountains, door latch defects, restroom fixtures, and HVAC cooling/heating failures.

🔌 Equipment

Faulty lab computers, broken oscilloscopes, projector malfunctions in lecture halls, and defective electrical outlets.

🧹 Sanitation

Overflowing trash bins, hazardous chemical spills in labs, unsanitary restrooms, and hallway clutter obstructing pathways.

🚨 Safety & Security

Flickering emergency lighting, broken handrails, exposed wiring, slippery walkways, and emergency call box issues.

How the System Works

1️⃣

Submit a Report

Fill out a quick form describing the problem, select a category, and submit your ticket.

2️⃣

Admin Triage

Campus administrators review the report, assign technicians, and set status to "In Progress".

3️⃣

Live Resolution

Receive automatic in-app alerts as repairs are completed and tickets marked "Resolved".
